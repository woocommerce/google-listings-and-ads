# Copy Guidelines

## Rule: Always Sentence Case

All GLA UI text uses sentence case — only the first word and proper nouns/acronyms are capitalized.

```text
Correct: "Connect your account"
Wrong:   "Connect Your Account"

Correct: "Save changes"
Wrong:   "Save Changes"

Correct: "Unable to reach Google"
Wrong:   "Unable To Reach Google"
```

## GLA Brand Names and Proper Nouns

These always keep their official casing regardless of position:

| Name | Use |
|---|---|
| Google for WooCommerce | "Set up Google for WooCommerce" |
| Merchant Center | "Connect your Merchant Center account" |
| Google Ads | "Create a Google Ads campaign" |
| Google Analytics | "Link Google Analytics" |
| Performance Max | "Performance Max campaigns" |
| WooCommerce | "Sync with WooCommerce" |
| WordPress | "WordPress settings" |
| Jetpack | "Connect via Jetpack" |

Acronyms are always uppercase: **API**, **URL**, **CSV**, **MC**, **GTIN**, **SKU**, **ID**, **SSL**.

## UI Element Examples

### Buttons
```text
✅ "Connect your account"
✅ "Save changes"
✅ "Continue"
✅ "Set up Merchant Center"
✅ "Create campaign"

❌ "Connect Your Account"
❌ "Save Changes"
❌ "Set Up Merchant Center"
```

### Labels and Inputs
```text
✅ "Merchant Center ID"
✅ "Target country"
✅ "Daily budget"
✅ "Ad destination URL"

❌ "Merchant Center Id"
❌ "Target Country"
❌ "Daily Budget"
```

### Headings and Page Titles
```text
✅ "Account settings"
✅ "Free product listings"
✅ "Paid campaigns"
✅ "Shipping settings"

❌ "Account Settings"
❌ "Free Product Listings"
❌ "Paid Campaigns"
```

### Notices and Messages
```text
✅ "Your account has been connected successfully."
✅ "Unable to sync products. Please try again."
✅ "No campaigns found."

❌ "Your Account Has Been Connected Successfully."
❌ "Unable To Sync Products."
```

### Navigation and Tabs
```text
✅ "Product feed"
✅ "Reports"
✅ "Settings"
✅ "Shipping"

❌ "Product Feed"
❌ "All Reports"
```

## When in Doubt

Capitalize only: first word of the string, proper nouns, and acronyms.

"Connect your Google Ads account and enter a daily budget (USD)." ← correct
